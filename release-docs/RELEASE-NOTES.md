# Kelly's Replay Mod 1.0

Record a mission, then replay it with a timeline, free and tracking cameras, camera paths, adjustable playback speed, lighting and weather tools. Capture the finished shot with OBS or another recording application.

## Highlights

- Per-replay camera projects save paths, point position, aim, roll and field of view. Select a point to return to it, and delete individual points.
- Record full sessions or save recent gameplay; check recording integrity and recover a separate copy of supported interrupted recordings.
- Delete one or several recordings from the library; deleted files move to a recovery folder.
- Fresh recordings capture more turret, cargo, parachute and engine state. Playback improvements include guided ship guns and deployed vehicles; the exact results depend on what the recording captured.
- Missiles no longer explode early or differently after each rewind; replayed explosions come only from the recording, including in existing replays.
- Fresh recordings capture missiles, rockets and bombs leaving the aircraft, including bay drops and rail launches, and restore them correctly when seeking.
- Space plays and pauses the replay even while the game's own camera panel is open.
- Studio sliders no longer keep changing when you fly the camera with A/D or the arrow keys after clicking one, and Space no longer repeats the last button you clicked.
- Copilot views, two-aircraft framing, fixed-angle follow, aircraft light controls, custom lights, camera collision controls and adjustable UI colours support shot making.

## Upgrading

1.0 is the public name even though the old tester version was numbered 2.16.4.3. Replace the old DLL manually and keep only one ReplayMod DLL installed. Keep your configuration and back up your recordings. New recordings use format 12 and cannot be opened by older ReplayMod versions. Older supported recordings remain readable, but missing historical data cannot be reconstructed.

A few reports remain under investigation, including foliage flicker, audio/visual differences, placed objects shown with the wrong rotation and an isolated replay-indexing crash whose exact cause is not confirmed. If a problem occurs, use Help → Support → Export logs and retain the replay.
